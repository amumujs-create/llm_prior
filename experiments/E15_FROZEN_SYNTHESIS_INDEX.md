# E15 Frozen Synthesis Index

## Status

E15-A through E15-D are a completed synthetic prior-utilization program.
No further E15 family, numerical calibration, policy, or outcome analysis is
authorized by this index.  The next research phase is external validation
(E16), not an extension of the synthetic grammar.

## Program-wide integrity boundary

Each experiment froze its generative family, policy definitions, numerical
geometry, seeds, corpus integrity, and result artifacts before outcome
interpretation.  The claims below apply only to the respective frozen
synthetic families and are not claims of universal optimality.

## Frozen claim hierarchy

### Strongest claim

> **Premature point commitment to unresolved futures can be costly.**

This recurred in E15-A, E15-C, and E15-D (including D3 point-collapse stress),
with E15-B showing the same retention benefit for weak scope evidence.

### Moderately supported claim

> **Evidence is most useful when it is aligned with the uncertainty dimension being reduced.**

E15-A aligned onset evidence with onset hypotheses; E15-C aligned residual
evidence with residual-shape hypotheses; E15-B demonstrated the countercase in
which endpoint evidence did not identify post-scope realizations.  E15-D
prospectively supported kappa-selective reduction but did not support its full
two-contrast conjunction.

### Retired claim

> **Weakly identified dimensions should never be weighted.**

This is not supported.  In particular, E15-D D2_S was direction-incompatible
with the predicted positive cost and inconclusive, rather than establishing
that additional weighting of weak lambda was harmful.

## Final E15 utilization principle

> **Preserve unresolved hypothesis diversity, use evidence to guide reduction
> along the dimensions it informs, and avoid premature point collapse.**

This principle is conditional: it does not prescribe a universal threshold,
does not claim that every weakly identified dimension must never be weighted,
and does not establish behavior outside the four frozen synthetic families.

## Next phase

E16 is an external-validation program.  Its role is to test whether the
frozen E15 principle survives outside the synthetic grammar, beginning with a
mechanistic external system (E16-A) and then a real tabular/process
extrapolation setting (E16-B).  E16 must carry the E15 principle forward as a
predeclared hypothesis rather than rediscover it or enlarge E15.
