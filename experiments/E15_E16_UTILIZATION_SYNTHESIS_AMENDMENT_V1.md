# E15–E16 Utilization Synthesis Amendment v1

**Status: post-confirmatory interpretation amendment.** E15 remains a frozen
synthetic program; this document does not alter its protocols or results.

E15 established, within its frozen synthetic families, that premature point
collapse can be costly and that evidence must align with the uncertainty
dimension being reduced. E16-B supplies a prospective external boundary:
uniform preservation over a frozen operational reference measure did not beat
validation-selected MAP in real CCPP high-AT compound covariate shift.

The combined claim hierarchy is therefore:

1. **Supported within E15 families:** premature point commitment can be costly.
2. **Supported across E15 and refined by E16-B:** evidence should guide
   reduction along uncertainty dimensions it informs.
3. **Not supported as a general rule:** uniformly preserving every admissible
   hypothesis is robustly superior to point commitment.

The revised working principle is:

> Preserve uncertainty where justified, but allocate probability mass using
> evidence about the relevant uncertainty dimension; neither indiscriminate
> uniform diversity nor unconditional point collapse is a universal policy.
