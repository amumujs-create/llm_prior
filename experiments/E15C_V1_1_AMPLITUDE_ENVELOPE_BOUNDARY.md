# E15-C v1.1 amplitude-envelope interpretation boundary

E15-C v1.1 conditions on the following known structure:

\[
\text{exact invariant}
\quad+\quad
\text{known residual-amplitude envelope}
\quad+\quad
\text{uncertain residual shape}.
\]

Every residual policy uses the same admissible amplitude bound,

\[
A\in[-1.5A_{\rm ref},+1.5A_{\rm ref}].
\]

Therefore the primary C1/C2 contrasts isolate residual-**shape** uncertainty conditional on that common envelope. They do not characterize unconstrained residual-amplitude uncertainty, and E15-C v1.1 must not be described as supplying no residual information beyond the invariant.

The bounded profile is materially active in the discarded C0 v1.1 calibration: cap activation fractions were low `.534`, medium `.300`, and high `.247`. The v1-to-v1.1 protected quota reduction, from 184,957 to the minimum-quota result of 125, is consequently an estimator-feasibility correction under the known admissible envelope, not evidence that residual-shape uncertainty disappeared.

The confirmatory questions remain separate:

1. **C1/C2:** how to allocate unresolved residual-shape uncertainty within the shared envelope.
2. **Closed mechanism:** the stress consequence of treating a valid but incomplete invariant as complete (`A=0`).
3. **Matched-free backbone:** the incremental predictive value of sharing the correct invariant while preserving matched residual capacity.

The minimum-quota outcome means only that the protected variance requirement did not exceed the predeclared minimum of 125; it does not claim that 125 is a variance-optimal task count.
