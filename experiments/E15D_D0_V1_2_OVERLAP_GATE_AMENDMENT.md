# E15-D0 v1.2 overlap-gate amendment

## Status and scope

This pre-outcome amendment follows the failed, discarded E15-D0 v1.1
construct-calibration attempt. No E15-D predictive policy outcome, contrast,
sign, rank, or winner has been calculated or inspected.

## Single change

E15-D0 v1.2 removes only the two adjacent cross-sectional distribution-overlap
requirements from construct selection:

\[
Q_{95}(E_\kappa^L)>Q_{05}(E_\kappa^S),\qquad
Q_{95}(E_\lambda^S)>Q_{05}(E_\lambda^J).
\]

They are not replaced by a new threshold. The core median evidence-vector
gates remain the complete staged-identification definition.

## Rationale

The v1.1 D0 artifact rejected all numerically finite, continuation-distinct
candidate geometries through these overlap requirements. Cross-sectional
5–95% overlap is not a scientific component of the prospective construct:
L/S/J remain continuous by construction because they are nested prefixes of
the same smooth polynomial trajectory and master noise path. Requiring both a
substantial staged median shift and mandatory central-band overlap imposed an
unnecessary tension on the intended construct.

## Explicitly unchanged

The following are unchanged: prior family; supplied terms; evidence definition;
sum-NLL; support, grid, noise, exposure, and far-horizon ladders; selection
priority; all core median gates; continuous-RMS clique threshold; D1/D2
definitions; and prospective predictions \(D1_D<0\), \(D2_D>0\), and,
conditional on J geometry, \(D2_D^J<0\).

## Fresh discarded sample

The v1.1 discarded calibration sample is permanently retired as a failed
construct-calibration attempt. Version 1.2 uses exactly task IDs 0–399 under
the new namespace `e15d-d0-v1.2-discarded`; no v1.1 latent task or seed is
reused. If v1.2 again has no selected candidate, a future v1.3 may alter only
one numerical ladder axis in response to the dominant core-gate failure.
